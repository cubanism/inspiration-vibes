The vibe · أهلاً وسهلاً

Hospitality is our sound system

Inspiration Vibes is an Amman-based event management and DJ collective. We grew up on dabke lines, wedding zaffas and Amman's rooftop summers — and we bring that same warmth to every stage we build.

From the first invitation to the last track, we handle the details so you can be present for all of it.

01

Event design

Concepts, venues, vendors and guest experience — designed around your night, not a template.

02

DJ & sound

Selectors and sound systems that read the room: dabke, Arabic pop, house, techno and everything between.

03

Production

Lighting, staging, visuals and the small details that make a room feel electric.
